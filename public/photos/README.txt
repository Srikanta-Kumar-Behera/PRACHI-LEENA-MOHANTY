Put your photographs in this folder, then point to them in src/config.js.

Example:
  photo: "/photos/first-meeting.jpg"

Any photo left empty (or a path that does not exist) shows a soft pink
"Add your photo" placeholder instead, so nothing ever looks broken.

Tips
- Landscape or square photos work best for the story cards.
- Portrait or square photos work best for the polaroids.
- Keep each file under about 400 KB (resize to roughly 1200 px wide) so the
  site loads fast on a phone.
